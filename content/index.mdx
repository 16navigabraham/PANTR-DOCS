# Pantr

**Pantr** is a Telegram-first crypto trading assistant powered by Claude AI. Users chat with the bot in natural language to buy, sell, and track tokens on **Solana**, **Base**, and **Mantle**.

## What Makes Pantr Different

- **Conversational AI-first** — every interaction is routed through Claude. Say "buy $10 of BONK" and it happens.
- **Multi-chain** — native support for Solana (Jupiter), Base (Odos, KyberSwap, Uniswap V3/V4), and Mantle.
- **Alpha callers** — subscribe to users in TG groups whose contract addresses trigger automatic buys for you.
- **Auto-sell** — set take-profit and stop-loss and let the bot do the work (premium).
- **Privy-managed wallets** — no private key handling, no seed phrase exposure. Keys live in a TEE.

## Quick Start

1. Open Telegram, search for the bot, and send `/start`
2. The bot creates Solana + Base wallets for you automatically
3. Deposit SOL or ETH to your wallet
4. Say "buy $10 of [contract address]" — Claude handles the rest
